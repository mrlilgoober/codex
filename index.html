🪕 Bear & Bird: Dad's Adventure

A fan tribute with a real level-based game loop.

Level: 1 Notes: 0 Feathers: 3 Time: 60

How to play

  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD
  • Collect every note to clear a level.
  • Avoid the witch — each hit costs one feather.
  • Beat all 3 levels before you run out of time/lives.
